# Everywhen

An intimate science-fiction mystery about a man who discovers that his life does not occupy time in the way he thought it did.

## Story bible

The bible is three connected documents. Each can be revised on its own, as long as all three stay consistent with one another.

| Document | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [01 Rules](bible/01-rules.md) | The hidden mechanism, the rules it obeys and what they forbid. Also where each of the three theories is locally right, and the open questions. |
| [02 Causal timeline and ledger](bible/02-causal-timeline.md) | The true chronology in calendar order, the three folds, the Knot script (minute by minute), and a ledger giving every artefact and phrase its origin, chain of custody and status. |
| [03 Character and revelation](bible/03-character-and-revelation.md) | The cast and the three accounts of the break-up. The seven anomalies, each with competing explanations. Revelation order by movement, fair-play plants, revelation tests and the open ending. |

**Markers:** **[canon]** is decided for this draft. **[provisional]** is a working assumption. **[open]** is left undecided on purpose.

## Development stages

1. ✅ Define the observable phenomena (document 03, §3)
2. ✅ Build the competing explanations (document 03, §3)
3. ✅ Design the causal structure (documents 01 and 02)
4. ⬜ Build the relationship around the mystery (a starting sketch is in document 03, §§1–2)
5. ⬜ Write the opening as a proof of concept

**The governing question:** what if the mystery isn't how a man can move through everywhen, but why he has spent his entire life believing that he couldn't?
